Programme

From inspiring lectures, installations, and exhibitions to the Fashion Show, Design KANTÝNA discussions, and other accompanying programs. Do you enjoy creating and meeting other designers? Workshops and creative studios await you throughout the week. Or you can take a piece of Zlin Design Week home with you from the Design Market. And when you need to unwind, there are concerts and parties going on all week long!

→ A packed program awaits you at Zlin Design Week! You can find tickets for the paid events here on the smsticket.cz website.
