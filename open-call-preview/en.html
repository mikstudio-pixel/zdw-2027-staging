Open Call 2027

Zlin Design Week is launching an Open Call for anyone who wants to help bring the festival to life. We want to create space for new connections and ideas that are too good to sit in a drawer.

Apply through our form and get involved in exhibitions, fashion performances and discussions, or share your knowledge and experience through workshops. Whether you work in a creative field and enjoy coming up with new ideas, or create your own design pieces you would like to sell at the Design Market, we welcome new connections and are happy to give your work space at the festival.

Download terms and conditions

You can submit your own project, collaboration proposal, or programming idea to the Open Call in one or more of the following categories:

Exhibitions
and installations

in public or festival spaces

Discussions and lectures

Fashion shows
or performances

Side events

workshops, guided tours, educational programs, community programs, etc.

Sales stall at the Design Market

I WANT TO SIGN UPYou don't need a finished project or to be a professional designer. All you need to do is describe as clearly as possible in the application form what you would like to present as part of the festival and what you envision the collaboration to look like.
